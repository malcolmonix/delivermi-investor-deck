01 · The Problem

A local business runs on six disconnected tools — and leaks value at every handoff.

How an African SME operates today (Fragmented Stack)

Zero shared data

1. Ad Spend

Boosted IG/TikTok posts with zero link to verified checkout — impossible to prove ROAS.

2. Manual DM Orders

WhatsApp chats & phone calls; once numbers swap, marketplaces lose 100% of repeat orders.

3. Unprotected Transfers

Buyers fear prepaying strangers; vendors get burned by fake bank alerts and failed COD trips.

4. Informal Dispatch

Unverified roadside riders blocked at estate gates, burning petrol with zero chain-of-custody.

5. Paper Stock Ledgers

Walk-in sales don't update online listings — causing paid orders on out-of-stock items.

6. Punitive Aggregators

Legacy food apps take 20–30% commission, forcing vendors to inflate online menu prices.

Where ₦10,000 of order value leaks today

Trust Deficit Suppresses Conversion

High-ticket electronics, wigs & catering stall online because neither side trusts the handoff.

Price Inflation Drives Buyers Offline

25% commissions make online ordering a luxury tax instead of a daily habit.

Invisible Cashflow = Zero SME Credit

Without verified inventory & settlement trails, profitable merchants cannot access working capital.
