The problem

Local commerce has demand — it breaks on trust.

Buyers fear prepaying

Strangers on Instagram and WhatsApp disappear after transfer. Merchants lose money to fake alerts and cancelled cash-on-delivery.

Platforms leak to WhatsApp

Once phone numbers are swapped, repeat orders leave the platform — and so does the revenue and the data.

Fees push prices up

20–30% delivery-app commissions force vendors to mark up online prices, so customers walk back to the counter.

Our solution

A closed loop — every step feeds the next.

    ChatMi

    Social feed, reels and private chat with contact details auto-redacted.

  2. 2 · Shop

    ShopMi

    PayMi Escrow

    Funds held safely — no cash, no fake alerts.

    Buyer's code releases payment to merchant, rider and partners at once.

Nationwide logistics

A hybrid network: our EV fleet for priority work, partner carriers for reach.
